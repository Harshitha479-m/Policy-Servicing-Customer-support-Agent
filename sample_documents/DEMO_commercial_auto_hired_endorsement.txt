Document: DEMO Hired and Non-Owned Auto Liability Endorsement CA-20

Hired Autos Coverage
This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the Commercial Auto Coverage Part. We will pay all sums an insured must pay as damages for bodily injury or property damage arising out of the maintenance or use of any hired auto by you or your employees in the course of your business operations.

Non-Owned Autos
Coverage extends to non-owned autos used in connection with your business operations by employees or authorized agents.

Limits and Conditions
Coverage under this endorsement is subject to a $500,000 combined single limit and applies as excess over any other valid and collectible insurance available to the driver or vehicle owner.
