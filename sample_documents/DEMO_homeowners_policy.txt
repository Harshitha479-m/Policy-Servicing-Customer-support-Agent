Section I - Property Coverages
Coverage A - Dwelling
We cover sudden and accidental direct physical loss to the dwelling shown in the declarations, subject to exclusions, conditions, limits, and the deductible shown in the policy schedule.

Water damage
Sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water from within a plumbing system is covered when the loss is not otherwise excluded. The policyholder must take reasonable steps to protect the property from further damage and keep receipts for reasonable emergency repairs.

Deductible
The all other perils deductible for the demonstration policy is $1,000. A deductible applies once per covered occurrence unless the applicable endorsement states otherwise.

Claims reporting
Report a loss as soon as reasonably possible through the claims contact listed in the policy administration record. This demonstration document does not contain a real telephone number.
