Document: DEMO Water Backup Endorsement 04

Water backup limit
Subject to all terms and exclusions, the demonstration policy provides a $10,000 limit for direct physical loss caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through sewers or drains, or overflows from a sump.

This endorsement does not expand coverage for losses caused by long-term seepage, wear and tear, or failure to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage. Refer to the policy wording and declarations for the complete terms.
