Document: DEMO Commercial Auto Policy Wording

Section I - Covered Autos
We cover autos designated in the policy declarations schedule.

Newly Acquired Autos
If you acquire an additional auto during the policy period, we will provide coverage for that auto for up to 14 days after you acquire it, provided all vehicles owned by the named insured are currently insured with us. The coverage provided during these 14 days will be the same as the broadest coverage provided on any auto currently listed on the policy schedule. To continue coverage beyond 14 days, the named insured or designated broker must report the newly acquired auto to underwriting and pay any additional required premium.

Section II - Liability Coverage
We will pay all sums an insured legally must pay as damages because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an accident resulting from the ownership, maintenance, or use of a covered auto.

Deductible and Physical Damage
Comprehensive and Collision coverages are subject to the deductibles stated in the policy administration record. A single deductible applies per covered collision loss.

Exclusions
Coverage does not apply to intentional injury, racing, unlisted drivers operating outside permissible business scope, or wear and tear.
