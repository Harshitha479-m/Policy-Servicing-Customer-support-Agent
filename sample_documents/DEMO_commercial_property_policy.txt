Document: DEMO Small Business Property Policy Wording

Section I - Covered Property
We cover Direct Physical Loss to Building and Business Personal Property located at the premises described in the declarations, caused by or resulting from any Covered Cause of Loss.

Deductible
We will not pay for loss or damage in any one occurrence until the amount of loss or damage exceeds the Deductible shown in the Declarations.

Certificates of Insurance
A Certificate of Insurance (COI) may be issued by an authorized agent or broker as evidence of coverage currently in force. A certificate does not alter, amend, or extend the coverage afforded by the policy. Certificates reflect the policy term, limits of insurance, and named insured as recorded in the administration system. For special wording, additional insured endorsements, or loss payee designations, formal underwriting endorsement request is required.

Notice of Cancellation
In the event of cancellation, notice will be provided to the first named insured in accordance with statutory requirements and policy conditions.
